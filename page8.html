The building's five brick towers are shaped in relation to the pools beneath them, creating distinct interior spaces while establishing a varied roofline. Constructed using approximately 300,000 handmade bricks, the towers contrast with the glazed lower structure, where windows frame views of the harbour and bring natural light into the building. A central feature is the Valley Pool, an outdoor pool positioned between the towers on the first floor, allowing visitors to swim under open skies. In Monarchs at Play, you choose from one of several monarchs, each of which has their own unique abilities and buildings. In a run, like most other city-builders, you start out small by placing what little buildings you have available to you, the ultimate goal being to place different kinds of tiles next to one another to build up combos.

Different buildings allow you to rack up resources, which in turn lets you place new buildings; you get how this works! You aren't doing this alone, however, as you have rivals to compete against, where you'll have to destroy the buildings they place to continue growing your own empire.
