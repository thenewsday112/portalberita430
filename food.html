#334. Trader Joe’s Is Opening 28 More Stores Across The U.S.—See If Your City Made The List

📅 Oct 24, 2026  |  Category: Food  |  Read Source →

Your next impulse tote purchase may be closer than you think.

We’re entering the stretch of the year when a Trader Joe’s run becomes less of a casual grocery trip and more of a holiday logistics operation. There are appetizers to stockpile, seasonal snacks you absolutely did not go in for, and probably another reusable tote that somehow ends up coming home with you anyway.

So the timing is especially good for shoppers in two cities. Trader Joe’s recently opened a new store in Anaheim Hills, California, on October 22, followed by another in West Jordan, Utah, on October 23. That gives both areas a brand new freezer aisle just in time for the Halloween-to-holiday-shopping sprint.

And Trader Joe’s isn’t slowing down after that. The chain currently has 28 more locations listed as coming soon across 15 states, though exact opening dates haven’t been announced for most of them (and some aren’t expected until 2027).

Trader Joe’s says it will update each store’s announcement page as opening dates become official. If this is your city’s first, you’re in for a treat.
